# Journal

*Six months of mornings: habits, mood, sleep.*

> [!stats]
> - **183** daily notes
> - **70%** days with a walk
> - **53%** with the gym
> - **12** day walking streak

Each daily note has a `habits` list and a mood, energy and sleep check-in. One row per habit, one square per week; brighter means more days that week. Below, one dot per day: height is mood, colour is energy, size is sleep.

![[Journal.base#Habits]]

## What I noticed

- Sleep shows up in the mood: 3.6 on average after 7.5 hours or more, 2.9 after less than 6.5.
- The gym is the habit that slips (53% of days); walking sticks (70%).
