# Agate demo vault

A made-up personal vault, organised like a real one (inbox, projects, areas, resources, journal), with charts on top of it. Everything is invented. The hike follows the Appalachian Trail in Georgia only roughly; its GPS tracks are generated and not for navigation.

## Two jobs, one vault

This vault is both the showcase and Agate's test bed (see `docs/adr/0002-one-vault.md` in the repo):

- **Showcase**: everything outside `tests/`. Polished area notes, the ones in the screenshots. Generated by `scripts/demo-vault.mjs`.
- **Tests**: `tests/`. One note per way a chart can appear, broken specs on purpose, the manual checklist and fixture data. Start at [[Test guide]]. Its bases only look inside `tests/`, and the showcase bases filter by `type`, which test notes don't set, so neither side's charts pick up the other's notes.

Every chart in both halves is rendered by the test suite (`npm test`).

Open [[Home]] for the list of area notes (each one a small dashboard), or any base in `bases/` and switch views from the toolbar.

Charts live in `charts/`; each is a plain Vega or Vega-Lite spec you can copy into your own vault. Click a mark that stands for a note to open it.

## Setup

From the Agate repo: `npm run build`, then `npm run install:local` (or `make demo` to also regenerate the showcase). Open this folder as a vault and trust the plugin. The hike basemap loads map tiles (turn on **Load remote data** in Agate settings); everything else is local.

Regenerate the notes and data with `node scripts/demo-vault.mjs` (fixed seed: same output every time).

## Looks

Each area note is designed for one of three looks (its `look` property):

| Look | Theme | Settings | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| elegant | Tufte, light | snippet `agate-demo` | [[Russian campaign 1812]], [[Reading]] |
| dark | Minimal, dark, Nord colour scheme (Minimal Settings), or Obsidian Nord | snippet `agate-demo` | [[AT Georgia 2025]], [[Interview prep]], [[Travel]], [[Journal]] |
| colourful | AnuPpuccin (Mocha) | snippet `agate-demo` | [[Board games]], [[Work]], [[Vault review]] |

Install the themes and plugins with `make extras` in the repo (themes AnuPpuccin, Tufte and Minimal; plugins Tufte Suite, Side-Notes and Minimal Settings; downloaded rather than committed because they keep their own licences), or from Settings. The vault opens in Tufte. [[Russian campaign 1812]] uses Tufte Suite's figure and margin-note callouts; they lay out in the margin in Reading view. The snippet in `.obsidian/snippets/`, `agate-demo`, turns the `[!stats]` callout into a row of big numbers and keeps figure callouts at text width. Charts follow light and dark mode on their own.
